Privacy Policy – RealSense SDK

Last Updated: September 1, 2026

RealSense ("we" or "our") is committed to respecting your privacy and protecting your personal information. This privacy policy ("Policy") explains how we collect and use information collected through our software development kits ("SDKs"), including limited Personal Information (as defined below) collected in connection with usage analytics, and it applies solely to such collection and use of information through the SDKs.

For the purposes of this Policy, "Personal Information" means information that identifies an individual or that may, with reasonable effort, be used to identify an individual. When you opt in to usage analytics, the only Personal Information we process is your IP address. We primarily collect and process information that cannot be associated with a specific individual, including: SDK version, build type (Debug/Release), and which build-time features were compiled in; operating system name and version, CPU architecture, GPU model family, and driver versions; camera model name, hardware variant, how many of each type are connected, and connection type; firmware version running on each connected device type; stream type, format, resolution, frame rate, and total streaming duration; which post-processing filters were used, their parameters, and how often each was applied; only options you actually changed from their default values; the category and count of firmware-level error notifications; session identifier, start time, duration, and how many sessions are bundled into a given upload; and an anonymous per-installation identifier generated randomly to distinguish usage patterns in aggregate statistics. However, if such information is combined with Personal Information, we will treat the resulting combined information as Personal Information.

We do not collect camera capture data of any kind, including depth frames, color or RGB images, infrared frames, point clouds, audio, or video; device serial numbers, ASIC serial numbers, or calibration data; hostname, or MAC address; your name, email address, or account information; stack traces, file paths, or user directory names; or any information that would enable us to link installations on different machines to the same individual.

You are under no legal obligation to consent to the collection of Personal Information described above. Any collection of Personal Information in connection with usage analytics is entirely voluntary, is disabled by default, and requires your explicit consent. You may enable or disable analytics at any time through the SDK settings and may withdraw your consent at any time without affecting your ability to use the SDK.

We collect and use the Personal Information and anonymous usage statistics solely to improve our product, enhance its functionality, and support research and development and ongoing business operations.

We do not sell Personal Information to any third party. We may share Personal Information with our service providers that perform services on our behalf and require access to certain Personal Information in order to provide those services.

Since we operate globally, it may be necessary to transfer data, including Personal Information, to jurisdictions other than your own. In these instances, we will ensure that the transfer aligns with the applicable data protection requirements.

We retain Personal Information only for as long as necessary to anonymize it and fulfill the purposes described in this Policy, as well as to comply with applicable legal obligations and resolve disputes.

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have certain rights regarding the Personal Information we process. These may include the right to access your Personal Information, correct inaccurate Personal Information, object to or restrict its processing, and withdraw your consent to such processing at any time.

If you wish to exercise any of the above rights or raise any concerns regarding our processing of your Personal Information, please contact us at [privacy@realsenseai.com]. We may request specific information from you to verify your identity and confirm your right to access your Personal Information (or to exercise any of your other rights). This is a security measure designed to ensure that Personal Information is not disclosed to any person who is not entitled to receive it. We may also contact you to request additional information in connection with your request in order to facilitate and expedite our response.

We may update this Policy from time to time at our sole discretion. The most current version will always be made available through our registration page and will be indicated by the "Last Updated" date appearing at the top of the Policy.
